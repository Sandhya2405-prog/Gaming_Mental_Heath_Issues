🎮 Gaming Habits and Their Impact on Human Health
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a gaming dataset to understand how video game usage and gaming habits affect the health and lifestyle of children, teenagers, and adults.

The analysis explores the relationship between gaming time, playing habits, and various health-related factors. It aims to identify potential negative effects of excessive gaming on physical health, mental well-being, sleep patterns, and daily activities.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze gaming habits and playing patterns among individuals.
Understand how excessive gaming can affect physical health.
Explore the relationship between gaming and mental health.
Identify the impact of gaming on sleep schedules.
Study how gaming influences daily routines and productivity.
Discover patterns and insights through data analysis and visualization.
📊 Key Areas of Analysis

The project investigates the following areas:

Gaming Behavior

🔍 Expected Insights

Through this analysis, the project aims to identify:

Whether longer gaming hours are associated with health-related concerns.
How gaming habits may influence sleep quality.
Whether excessive gaming is associated with reduced physical activity.
How gaming patterns relate to stress and mental well-being.
Which age groups may be more vulnerable to unhealthy gaming habits.
📈 Project Outcome

The main goal of this project is to transform raw gaming data into meaningful insights that help people understand the possible health effects of gaming.

The findings can help promote awareness about responsible gaming, balanced screen time, healthy sleeping habits, and an active lifestyle.

⚠️ Disclaimer

This project is intended for educational and data analysis purposes only. Any relationships identified in the dataset should not automatically be interpreted as proof of causation or medical conclusions.
